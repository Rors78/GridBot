# GridBot audit: 2026-09-23

A read-only audit of D:\GridBot: gridbot.py, oracle.py v3.1 (read only), test_gridbot.py, tui\, and the .bat/.ps1 launchers. No code was changed. Four parallel reviews were run: trading/accounting, reliability/ops, strategy edge, and capital/risk. The headline claims were re-checked by hand.

## 0. Answer

**The code is sound, but the strategy loses money in the one era we can measure, and the scanner's yield number is not a forecast.**

- **The books reconcile.** Both live grids reconcile to the cent (cash + coins × price = alloc + P/L), checked by hand twice.
- **The tests pass.** 132/132, and they touch only temp dirs.
- **The replay loses money.** 2,502 grids on 14 Kraken pairs, 2025-01-01 → 2026-09-11, using the unmodified `oracle.evaluate` to pick bands and gridbot's own `Grid` to trade them:
  - **−1.25% of alloc per 10 days** (mean; median −0.02%, win rate 49%).
  - The scanner predicted **+2.79%/day**.
- **The scanner's forecast doesn't hold up** in that replay:
  - It counts about **9.75× too many fills** (median, n=1,501).
  - Its score ranks outcomes no better than chance (Spearman −0.10).
  - Grids that passed its gates did no better than those that failed (−1.44% vs −1.17%).
  - It picked 8 levels in 2,518 of 2,522 evaluations: in practice its only choice is "widest band, fewest levels".
- **Pierce measurement (the user's stated gate):**
  - Share of grids pierced: 14% within 24h, 38% within 72h, 75% within 240h. The median time to a pierce is 71h.
  - A downward pierce costs a median **−5.4%** of alloc (p10 −9.1%, n=1,054).
  - The loss grows with band width, roughly **−0.27 × span**. For the live ~51% spans that is about **−14%** per pierce. The live `floor_pnl` agrees: TRUMP −10.8%, TIA −16.8%.
- **The 300 s exit rule and holding through have the same mean** (−1.25% each). The rule roughly halves the worst 1% (−11.1% vs −21.4%). It costs about 1.9 points in flat markets (+0.25% vs +2.13%, n=508).

**Replay caveats:**
- Only 6 of the 14 pairs are in the live watchlist, and TRUMP and TIA aren't in the data.
- Live bands are about 2.3× wider than in the replay.
- The era was a falling alt market (average 10-day return −2.2%).
- The exit was approximated as the first 15m close outside the band, which exits less often than the live 300 s rule.
- Spread was fixed at 0.10%.
- The effective n is about 200, because starts overlap and the pairs move together.
- The Grid was fed 4 points per 15m bar, so fills are undercounted on both the income and inventory sides.

The direction is solid. The magnitudes are soft. **The next measurement:** rerun with trades from D:\GridPick\data\trades at the print level.

Replay code and results are in this folder: `wf.py` (replay; it writes to the job tmp path hard-coded in `OUT`, so edit that before rerunning), `an*.py` (analysis), `wf.pkl` (raw evaluations), `f.pkl` (per-grid frame), and `cfg.pkl`.

The live sample is 2 grids, 2 fills and 0 round trips. That confirms nothing yet.

**Found in the TUI ported earlier today (my work, not pre-existing GridBot defects):**
- **The console never shows** `catching_up`, `last_print_age_s`, `unrestored`/`grids_held`, `feed.errors`/`last_error`, or `scanner.running`/`note`. Those are exactly the "stopped trading but looks fine" states. The "ws" age comes from Kraken heartbeats, so it stays fresh even when one pair's stream is dead.
- **The status file's local-time `ts` is read with `fold=0`.** On 2026-11-01 (DST) the console will show the bot as dead for an hour. Fix: add `ts_epoch` to status.json.
- **`STALE_S = 5`** against a status write roughly every 5.3 s makes the indicator flicker "stale" about 14% of the time. Fix: raise it to about 12.
- **"exit at 300s" is hard-coded** instead of following `--exit-after`.

**Kill launcher (by request, not a bug):**
- **Each click costs:** a full rescan, a REST catch-up, reset feed counters, lost caches, and a window where scan.json may be half-written (oracle.py:1206 is not atomic, and oracle.py stays untouched).
- **The clean shutdown path has never run:** the journal has 18 START rows and 0 STOP rows. Of today's restarts, 09:55 and 10:01 were test clicks during the TUI work and 09:57 was the user's.
- **Graceful variant that keeps "click = kill + relaunch":** gridbot_stop.ps1 first drops a stop file (or sends Ctrl-Break), waits about 5 s for the STOP row, then uses `-Force`.

**Checked and correct:**
- **Fill model:** trade-through at the level price is conservative; one print can fill several rungs but can't fill back and forth; fills are de-duplicated by trade id across replays.
- **Cash:** it can't go negative, since steps are at least 0.66%, over twice the maker fee.
- **Order-size checks:** `snap_levels` and the Kraken minimums are correct.
- **Single instance:** a double click and the boot race still end with one bot, because the lock holds.
- **Disk growth:** fine. The logs are capped.

## 6. Trade-level replay (audit/replay2, 2026-09-23)

**No variant makes money.** Every Kraken print was fed through frozen copies of gridbot.py and oracle.py. The exit engine matched the real `Bot._apply` to the cent on 119 of 119 grids. 2,502 grids, 14 pairs, 2025-01 to 2026-09. Mean P/L at 240h, % of alloc (re-checked from rp_frame.pkl):

| Variant | Mean | Median | Worst 1% | Win % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V0 current (300 s exit) | −1.24 | 0.00 | −11.2 | 49 |
| V1 hold | −0.87 | +0.66 | −21.0 | 56 |
| V2 hard stop −8% / −12% | −1.58 / −1.59 | 0.00 / +0.48 | −8.4 / −12.4 | 50 / 54 |
| V3 buy cap 2 / 3 rungs | −1.18 / −1.23 | −0.29 / −0.05 | −10.6 / −11.1 | 49 / 49 |
| V4 band k=3 / k=6 | −0.85 / −0.90 | −1.02 / −1.33 | −4.5 / −6.6 | 35 / 41 |
| V5 re-center after exit | −0.96 | −1.38 | −18.7 | 40 |
| k3 / k6 + re-center | −18.2 / −4.7 | | | 2 / 21 |

- **No 90% CI is above zero** (pair×month block bootstrap).
- **Hold vs current, same starts:** +0.37 pp, CI [−0.03, +0.84], with a worse tail. Not shown to be better.
- **Hard stops** are worse than both.
- **Narrow bands** look better per grid but die within 0.5–1.6 days. Per deployed day, or re-centered to stay deployed, they are the worst.
- **Fill counts:** the scanner overcounts 9.71× (the first replay said 9.75×, so the 4-point shortcut did not matter). 0.80 round trips per day realised against 10.4 predicted.
- **Downward pierce exit:** mean −5.89% of alloc (n=1,049). V0 holds capital about 5.2 of 10 days, so per deployed day it is −2.38% per 10 days.
- **Not supported by this data:** a rising market, pairs outside the 14 (TRUMP and TIA are not in the data), the live wider bands, redeploying into a new pick after an exit, and any claim of CI precision (the pairs move together).

**Conclusion:** in 2025–26, the scanner-picked neutral grid has no edge under any exit or band rule tried. Keep it on paper. More capital or live trading needs a different selection signal or a different market, not a different exit.
